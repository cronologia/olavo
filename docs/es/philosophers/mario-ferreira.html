Mário Dias Ferreira dos Santos (1907–1968)

Brazilian philosopher, translator and writer, creator of the system he named 'concrete philosophy'. In little more than fifteen years he published the monumental Enciclopédia de Ciências Filosóficas e Sociais, in dozens of volumes, working outside the university establishment. He fell into relative obscurity after his death and has been republished since the 2000s, with the rights to his work acquired by the publisher É Realizações.[3] [4] [5]

Vida y obra

AñoAcontecimiento
3 January 1907Born in Tietê (SP), son of the Portuguese Francisco Dias Ferreira dos Santos, a theatrical entrepreneur and cinema pioneer established in Pelotas (RS).[3] [5]
1913Appears, still a child, in his father's silent film 'Os óculos do vovô', one of the oldest Brazilian films known.[3]
1910s–1920s ≈Early schooling at the Ginásio Gonzaga, the Jesuit college of Pelotas; at 14 he wishes to enter the Society of Jesus but is counseled against it.[3]
1925–1930Studies at the Law Faculty of Porto Alegre, graduating in 1930; begins writing for newspapers of Pelotas and Porto Alegre (Diário de Notícias, Correio do Povo).[3]
1930s ≈Works as a journalist; after the Revolution of 1930, is jailed for criticism of the new regime.[3]
1940Hired by Livraria do Globo as a translator from Latin, Greek, German and French; translates Aristotle, Nietzsche, Kant, Pascal and Thomas Aquinas, among others.[3]
1945Moves to São Paulo, continuing his work as a translator at Editora Flama.[3]
1952Begins, with Filosofia e Cosmovisão, the publication of the Enciclopédia de Ciências Filosóficas e Sociais, the systematic project that would occupy the rest of his life (counts of the total vary: 45 and more than 50 volumes are both cited).[4]
1953–1956Publishes the encyclopedia's introductory volumes at speed: Lógica e Dialética (1953), Psicologia (1953), Teoria do Conhecimento (1954), Ontologia e Cosmologia (1954), Tratado de Simbólica (1956), Filosofia da Crise (1956), O Homem Perante o Infinito (1956) and Noologia Geral (1956).[4]
1957Publishes Filosofia Concreta, the tenth work of the series, in which he establishes his own way of philosophizing and the core of his system.[4]
11 April 1968Dies, leaving the encyclopedia almost entirely published and a vast body of work partly unpublished.[3] [4] [5]
2000s ≈His work returns to circulation: the publisher É Realizações acquires the rights and republishes his books, among them A Invasão Vertical dos Bárbaros (repub. 2012).[3] [5]
