Louis Lavelle

Louis Lavelle (1883–1951)

French philosopher, one of the principal French metaphysicians of the twentieth century and a representative of the 'philosophie de l'esprit' current. Professor at the Sorbonne and the Collège de France, he developed an ontology of participation in being, articulated in the tetralogy La dialectique de l'éternel présent.[1] [2] [3]
